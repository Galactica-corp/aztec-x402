## Major benefits unlocked

### Private, agent-native settlement

- **HTTP-native micropayments** — any API can be payment-gated with a three-phase commitment flow (402 challenge → server commitment → client finalization), compatible with agent runtimes.
- **Full transactional confidentiality** — AIP-20 private transfers on Aztec hide sender, receiver, and amount; spending patterns and negotiation positions are not leaked on a transparent ledger.
- **Structural counterparty assurance** — the commitment pattern binds recipient and completer at the protocol level, removing payment-attribution ambiguity that autonomous agents cannot resolve with discretionary judgment.
- **Replay integrity** — dual-layer anti-replay (one-shot nonces plus settled-txHash registry) suits unattended, high-frequency agent traffic.

### Stable value with privacy

- **Shielded wrapped stablecoin** — fiat price stability plus transactional confidentiality and Ethereum-anchored finality (deposit on L1, transact privately on L2, withdraw to L1).

### Fiat abstraction without crypto burden

- **Funding-source neutrality** — card, bank, or on-chain balance can fund the agent; the source stays invisible to principal and counterparty at the private settlement leg.
- **Merchant reach** — payout waterfall (private on-chain receipt, bank off-ramp, or just-in-time virtual card) extends acceptance to the card-accepting economy without requiring merchant crypto capability.
- **Near-zero marginal settlement cost** — private L2 stablecoin transfers compress fees vs. card interchange (~2–3%), enabling per-inference / per-query / per-API-call pricing.

### Compliance without surveillance

- **Privacy as a fiduciary requirement** — selective disclosure and ZK attestations satisfy regulatory obligations without a standing viewing key or network-level backdoor.
- **Mandate-ready autonomy** — signed spending limits and auditable agent execution reconcile autonomy with accountability.

### Expected outcome

Anyone — with or without crypto — can transact through autonomous agents at near-zero marginal settlement cost, with confidentiality preserved end-to-end across private legs and identity disclosure confined to regulated perimeters where law requires it.
